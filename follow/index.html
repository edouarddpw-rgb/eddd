        03 / Our solutions

        We don't justmove ads.We move attention.

        FOLLOW turns everyday scooter traffic into a network of moving brand touchpoints — then connects that real-world visibility to digital engagement you can measure.

        01

        Mobile advertising

        Transform scooters into moving brand touchpoints. Custom vehicle wrapping adapted to each advertiser's visual identity — visible while riding and while parked in strategic urban locations.

        02

        Hyperlocal visibility

        Reach people in the real world, in the areas where your business wants to be seen. Campaign planning can focus on relevant urban neighbourhoods and target destinations.

        03

        QR tracking & reporting

        Track QR-code engagement and receive campaign reporting every week. Where technically available and legally appropriate, we record aggregate engagement timing and geographic information.

        04
